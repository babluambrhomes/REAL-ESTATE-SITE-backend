
1. Har registered user by default Buyer

Registration ke time:

User
 │
 ├── Person
 │
 └── BuyerProfile

Example:

User	Person	BuyerProfile	SellerProfile	Organization Member
Monit	✅	✅	❌	❌
Sohan	✅	✅	❌	❌
Amit	✅	✅	❌	❌

Matlab registration = Buyer.

Condition 1 — User khud Individual Seller banna chahta hai

Monit bolta hai:

"Main individually property sell karna chahta hoon."

Tab:

Monit
 │
 ├── Person
 ├── BuyerProfile
 │
 └── SellerProfile
      sellerType = INDIVIDUAL
      userId = Monit
      organizationId = NULL

Table:

User	Buyer	Seller	Seller Type
Monit	✅	✅	INDIVIDUAL
Sohan	✅	❌	—
Amit	✅	❌	—

Yahan Monit khud seller entity hai.

Condition 2 — User Organization create karta hai

Sohan bolta hai:

"Mere paas ek company/organization hai. Main khud individual seller nahi hoon."

To:

Sohan (User)
 │
 ├── Person
 ├── BuyerProfile
 │
 └── OrganizationMember
       role = OWNER
            │
            ▼
       Sohan Company
            │
            └── SellerProfile
                 sellerType = ORGANIZATION

Database:

users
id	name
U1	Monit
U2	Sohan
organizations
id	name	createdBy
O1	Sohan Company	U2
seller_profiles
id	sellerType	userId	organizationId
SP1	INDIVIDUAL	U1	NULL
SP2	ORGANIZATION	NULL	O1

Important: Sohan ko individual SellerProfile nahi mila.

SellerProfile Sohan Company ki hai, Sohan ki nahi.

Condition 3 — Organization ke employees

Ab Sohan Company mein:

Sohan → OWNER
Amit → AGENT
Ravi → STAFF
Neha → ADMIN

Ye sab Organization Members hain.

Sohan Company
│
├── SellerProfile
│
├── Sohan
│    └── OWNER
│
├── Amit
│    └── AGENT
│
├── Ravi
│    └── STAFF
│
└── Neha
     └── ADMIN

Table:

organization_members
user	organization	role
Sohan	Sohan Company	OWNER
Amit	Sohan Company	AGENT
Ravi	Sohan Company	STAFF
Neha	Sohan Company	ADMIN

In employees ko automatically SellerProfile nahi milegi.

Because:

Employee ≠ Seller

Seller hai:

Sohan Company
     ↓
SellerProfile

Employee sirf company ko operate karta hai.

Aur sabse important: Employee bhi Buyer hai

Amit jab normal registration karega:

Amit
 │
 ├── Person
 └── BuyerProfile

Baad mein Sohan usko invite kare:

Amit
 │
 ├── BuyerProfile
 │
 └── OrganizationMember
       │
       └── Sohan Company
             role = AGENT

Amit Buyer bhi hai + Organization Member bhi hai.

Usko seller banne ki zarurat nahi hai.

Simple rule yaad rakho
Entity	SellerProfile?	BuyerProfile?
Normal User	❌	✅
Individual Seller	✅	✅
Organization Owner	❌*	✅
Organization	✅	❌
Organization Employee	❌	✅
Organization Agent	❌	✅

* Organization Owner ko individual SellerProfile nahi milegi, jab tak woh separately individual seller na bane.

Iska core principle

User = person/account

BuyerProfile = har registered user ka buyer identity

SellerProfile = actual selling entity

Organization = company/business entity

OrganizationMember = user ka company ke andar relationship

Is design mein "Sohan" aur "Sohan Company" ko mix nahi kiya jayega. यही आपके पूरे system का सबसे important separation है.
